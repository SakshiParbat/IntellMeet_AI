⚡ How It Works

Simple to start,
powerful at scale

1

Create or Join a Meeting

Start instantly with a unique meeting link, or schedule in advance. Invite teammates via email or share a join code. No downloads needed — works right in the browser.

2

AI Listens and Transcribes in Real Time

As your meeting happens, IntellMeet AI transcribes every word with speaker labels, captures key decisions, and highlights important moments automatically.

3

Get AI Summary & Action Items Instantly

The moment your meeting ends, receive a concise summary with all action items, owners, and deadlines — already added to your project board, ready to track.

4

Track, Collaborate & Improve

Monitor task progress on your Kanban board, review meeting analytics, and watch your team's productivity improve meeting by meeting.
